# User Research

[← Back to README](README.md)

## Research Goal

We needed to understand the challenges related to both time management and the numerous reminders that caregivers must keep track of regarding the care and maintenance of older adults. This would enable us to create an application that handles the difficult aspects for our target audience.

## Method

- **Technique:** Semi-structured interviews, 5 to 7 minutes. In these interviews, caregivers were asked several questions about their current situation and the challenges they face when having to manage the various medications that must be administered to older adults, including the organizational techniques they use for such situations.
- **Dates:** 20 - 22 of November 2026
- **Why this method:** This method was chosen because we felt we needed genuine feedback from the target audience to properly develop the application's features; face-to-face interviews allow for honest and direct responses. Mindful of time constraints, we decided to keep the interviews brief so that caregivers would not lose time answering questions when they could otherwise be working.

## Participants

| ID | Profile |
|----|---------|
| P1 | Patient's family member and primary caregiver. She takes care of his mother. 6 medications per week. |
| P2 | Member of a nursing home. Cares for several older adults, each of whom takes multiple medications. |
| P3 | Family member and caregiver. Cares for his father, who has Alzheimer's. Involves multiple medications and doctor visits. |
| P4 | Family member and caregiver. Cares for her father, who has Alzheimer's. Involves multiple medications and doctor visits. |

## Key Findings

### Finding 1

- **What we saw:** We observed that Participant 1 had difficulty remembering which medications were needed or determining when it was time to administer the appropriate medication to the patient.
- **Evidence:** "Honestly, I don't know how I'm going to manage; my memory just isn't what it used to be, you know? I feel like one of these days I'm going to forget the right medication."
- **Implication:** Our application needs a method to remind users which medication their patient is due for and when, thereby making their job easier.

### Finding 2

- **What we saw:** We observed that Participant 2, who had several patients under their care, almost always forgot whether they had already attended to a particular patient and had to rely on various methods and objects to remember.
- **Evidence:** "It is very difficult to keep track of all my patients; honestly, I think I sometimes forget one and have to go back and check, which takes up my time."
- **Implication:** Our application needs a way to handle multiple patients consistently, ensuring that no one is left without care.

### Finding 3

- **What we saw:** We observed that, given all the care the patient required, their caregiver was unable to keep an accurate record of when events took place.
- **Evidence:** It's always difficult to remember when it's time for medication. Especially now, when things aren't the way they used to be.
- **Implication:** The application needs reminders for both the caregiver and the patient.

## Customer Journey

1. The caregiver receives the patient's medical prescription.
2. The caregiver registers the patient, along with their medications and the frequency with which they must be taken.
3. The application reminds you when and which medication each patient is due to receive.
4. The app reminds you when it is time to buy new medication as your current supply runs low.
5. The application restores the missing medication with the press of a button, allowing you to continue using it without any issues.

## How Research Shaped the Solution

1. The caregiver forgot that he needed to buy more medicine for the patient. The patient's medication card changes color and begins sending notifications when the supply is running low.
2. The caregiver needs to manage multiple patients and their respective medications. The application features a search system for the patient list, as well as a personalized photo and personalized information for each patient.
3. The caregiver does not remember which medication is needed or at what time. The application simplifies this into a list organized by the next upcoming dose.
