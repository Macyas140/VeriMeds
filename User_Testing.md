# User Testing

[← Back to README](README.md)

## User 1

The first tester is a family member who is responsible for the care of their father, who suffers from Alzheimer's.

**Task to be completed:**

- The tester was asked to register a new patient, including the medications they need to take, the dosage for each, and the frequency with which they must be taken.
- It was requested that one of the medications have a low number of doses, in order to test the notifications.

**Results:**

- The tester was able to register the patient along with their respective medications.

**Improvements:**

- A visual feature was implemented that changes the color of the medication card when only a few doses remain.

---

## User 2

The second tester is a worker at a nursing home for the elderly, where he has several patients to look after.

**Task to be completed:**

- They were asked to register most of their patients in the application, along with their medications.

**Results:**

- The tester was able to register all of his patients.
- The tester encountered difficulties when searching for a specific patient, as there is no way to filter them.

**Improvements:**

- A search bar system is implemented to filter patients by name.

---

## User 3

The third tester is an older adult who had already been registered, including details on their medications and the times at which they are taken.

**Task to be completed:**

- The tester was asked to use the application for one day. We requested that they use it and take their medication whenever the application provided a reminder.

**Results:**

- The tester took his medications at the scheduled time, following the entire list of medications for the day.

**Improvements:**

- The notification system was improved to work even when the application is closed and the phone is disconnected.
