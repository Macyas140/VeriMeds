## Problem

The problem we aim to address with this application is the complexity involved in caring for an elderly person, which typically requires managing a large number of medications, each with its own specific dosage and administration schedule, while also keeping track of remaining supplies to ensure timely replenishment. Caregivers often rely on paper records, their own memory, or informal notes to manage these responsibilities. This becomes even more challenging when caring for multiple patients, increasing the likelihood that a caregiver might miss a dose, administer a medication twice, or fail to purchase more when a supply runs out, ultimately disrupting the patients' treatment regimen.

## Target Audience

Our target audience is divided into two groups.

1. **Caregivers of older adults (primary group).** Family members or individuals whose specific job is to care for the patient. They are responsible for keeping track of the medications patients take, when they take them, and when it is time to purchase more, whether for a single patient or for several.
2. **Older adults or patients who require special care.** The application handles registering them and keeping track of when they need to take their medication and which medication they should take. In this way, patients can help streamline the process, as they will receive reminders about when to take their medicine.

## Solution

Our solution is a mobile application where a caregiver keeps all patients and their medications in one place, with a clear view of how many doses remain and when it is necessary to purchase more medications for the patients.

It helps the caregiver maintain order when looking after multiple patients. The app utilizes a system of phone notifications to ensure that both the older adults and their caregivers are reminded exactly when it is time for a dose.

## How to Use

To use the application, one must be a family member, a staff member at a care facility, or a professional caregiver. Once registered, a caregiver can add the individual they are looking after (the patient) to the system, optionally including a photograph. The caregiver then logs the specific medications, dosages, and schedules for the patient.

Following this, the caregiver receives notifications when it is time to attend to the older adult, while the patient receives alerts indicating when it is time to take their medication and which specific medication they need to take.
